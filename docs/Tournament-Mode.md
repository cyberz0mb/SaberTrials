# Tournament mode

The tournament build supports single-elimination brackets for individual
players and teams. Players register with verified accounts, join the assigned
arena or team, and mark themselves ready. The bracket lists upcoming, active
and completed matches.

Individual matches use ten scored rounds and a deciding round when tied.
Team matches use TFFA scoring, with kill targets of 20, 30, 40 and 50 for 2v2
through 5v5. Missing players pause the match until the lineup is restored or
the reconnect allowance expires. An eligible reserve can replace an absent
starter. Authorized staff can pause, resume, restart or disqualify an entry.

Running an online event requires a matching Tournament-enabled server, client
modules and verified account integration.
