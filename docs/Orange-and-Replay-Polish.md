# Orange and match replays

Orange is a single-saber style with its own four-attack chain. It uses a
separate ready-stance preference and supports either dominant hand. Equipment
and animation eligibility are checked before the style is activated. Dual and
staff sabers retain their own combat systems.

Match highlights and deciding-strike replays hold the final shot for 2.5 seconds
within the overall 15-second limit. Viewers can skip independently. When footage
is unavailable, the client shows a result card.
