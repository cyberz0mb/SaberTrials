# Playing in a tournament

Sign in, open the Tournament screen, and register for the event. The bracket
shows match assignments. Join your assigned match and select **Ready** once in
the arena or on the assigned team. If a match pauses for a missing player,
rejoin and mark yourself ready again.

In team events, an eligible reserve can take an absent starter's place.
The [Tournament guide](Tournament-Mode.md) describes scoring and match rules.
