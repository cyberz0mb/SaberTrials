SABER TRIALS - CREDITS AND THIRD-PARTY ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS
======================================================

Saber Trials exists to contribute to the Jedi Academy modding community.
Credit should follow the work: when code, art, design, documentation, or a
technical approach from another project is used, that contribution must be
recorded here before the related change is released.


SABER TRIALS
------------

Project creation, direction, gameplay design, testing, and community vision:
The Saber Trials project creator.

Additional contributors will be listed by the public name or handle they ask
us to use. Contribution history will also remain visible in Git commits and
pull requests.

ASSET POLICY
------------

Do not add assets copied from Jedi Academy's base data, another mod, or an
online source unless redistribution permission and attribution are known.
Code licensing does not automatically grant permission to redistribute every
model, texture, sound, font, map, or other asset shipped alongside a project.

For each future third-party addition, record:
  - Project and author or contributor name
  - Source URL and exact commit or release
  - Files or feature used
  - License or written permission
  - Local Saber Trials files containing the derived work
